## Sounds
Every shipped sound file must be CC0 (freesound.org with the CC0 filter) or recorded by the author; list each one here with its author and link.

Placeholders until then: the mod ships no sound files. `assets/catgirl/sounds.json` points at vanilla Minecraft sounds, which the game already has:

| Sound event | Plays | Replace with |
|---|---|---|
| `catgirl:player.meow` | `minecraft:mob/cat/meow1..3`, pitched up | 3 CC0 meows |
| `catgirl:block.cat_bed.purr` | `minecraft:mob/cat/purr1..3` | 2 CC0 purrs |
| `catgirl:entity.bakeneko.hiss` | `minecraft:mob/cat/hiss1..3`, pitched down | 2 CC0 hisses |
| `catgirl:entity.cat_spirit.fade` | `minecraft:mob/cat/purreow1..2`, pitched up | 2 CC0 ghostly purrs |
| `catgirl:item.bell_collar.jingle` | `minecraft:note/icechime`, `minecraft:note/bell`, pitched up | 2 CC0 small bells |
